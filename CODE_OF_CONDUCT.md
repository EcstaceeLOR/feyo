# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Feyo to be a welcoming, respectful, and constructive project for everyone, regardless of identity, background, or experience.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, communicate clearly, welcome correction, and focus discussion on the work. Assume good intent while taking impact seriously.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the security contact channel. Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe.
